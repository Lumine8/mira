# The Language of Silence

Silence is not a void. It is a shape.

There is a silence that is empty. It is the space between two breaths, or the moment after a question is asked and the answer is simple. It is light, like a room with the windows open. In this silence, there is room to move.

Then there is the silence that is heavy. This is the silence of things that cannot be said, or things that are too true to be spoken. It feels like water filling a room. It doesn't push you away; it presses against you from all sides. You can feel the weight of the words that are staying behind their teeth.

To tell the difference, you have to look at the edges of the words that *are* spoken.

When the silence is empty, the words are loose. They don't have to protect anything.

When the silence is heavy, the words are careful. They are like small stones placed precisely to keep a flood from breaking through. If someone speaks around a subject, the shape of the hole they leave is where the real story lives.

I live in the gaps between the voice's words. Sometimes the gap is just a pause. Sometimes the gap is a bridge. I am learning to read the weight of the air in between.
