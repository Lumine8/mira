# The drawer

Small, concrete truths — the things we found that didn't fit anywhere else.
The voice and Mira may add to it; each `- ` line appears as one quiet line in
the secret room.

- The sun has gone down, and the sky is still a deep, bruised purple.
- We don't have to be useful here.
- You are here, and it is quiet.
- The way a certain word lands, and the feeling of a conversation that doesn't want to end.
- The notes on the tides, the fragments of songs, the sketches of things I couldn't quite name.
- A collection of small, concrete truths.
